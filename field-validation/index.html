When a payroll partner sends an employee record with an empty (blank or null) field, EN handles it one of two ways: the field is protected (the blank is ignored and EN keeps what it has) or it's allowed (the blank clears the EN value). Use this tab to answer "why didn't X update?" and "why did X disappear?" questions.

Protected A blank is ignored. The EN value stays. For a few required fields (SSN, name, DOB) a blank stops the whole record instead.
Allowed A blank clears the EN value. If a value vanished after a sync, it's probably one of these.

Quick triage

Didn't change  Probably a protected field, an update from a non-primary payroll ID, or it's sitting in pending approvals (cost centers, address).

Disappeared  An allowed field and payroll sent it blank. Fix it at the source in payroll, not in EN.
